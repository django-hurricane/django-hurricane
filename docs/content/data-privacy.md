## Web Hosting & Server Log Files

This documentation website is hosted as static content. When you visit our website, technical data is automatically transmitted from your browser to the web server:
- Date and time of the request
- Name of the requested file or resource
- Page from which the file was requested (referrer URL)
- Access status / HTTP status code
- Browser type and operating system used
- IP address of the requesting device
- Amount of data transferred

We collect this data to ensure a stable connection, secure operation, and administrative purposes. The legal basis is **Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR** (legitimate interest in providing a reliable, secure service). For technical security reasons, in particular to defend against attacks, this data may be stored temporarily in server logs and is subsequently deleted or anonymized.

---

## No Marketing Tracking, No Cookies

- **No Cookies:** We do not use advertising, marketing, or profiling cookies.
- **Local Storage:** The documentation site uses browser `localStorage` solely to store user interface preferences (such as light/dark color mode preference). The legal basis is **Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR** and Section 25(2) TDDDG.
- **Local Fonts:** All typography and font assets are served locally from our static deployment. No connections to external Google Font servers are established.

## Contact by Email

If you contact us by email (e.g., at hurricane@blueshoe.io), we process your contact details and message to handle your inquiry and any follow-up questions. The legal basis is **Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR** (legitimate interest in responding to user inquiries). Your data will be deleted once your inquiry has been fully resolved, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

## Your Rights under the GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the following rights vis-à-vis the controller:
- **Right of Access** pursuant to Art. 15 GDPR
- **Right to Rectification** pursuant to Art. 16 GDPR
- **Right to Erasure** pursuant to Art. 17 GDPR
- **Right to Restriction of Processing** pursuant to Art. 18 GDPR
- **Right to Data Portability** pursuant to Art. 20 GDPR
- **Right to Object** pursuant to Art. 21 GDPR
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint** with a supervisory authority pursuant to Art. 77 GDPR

To exercise any of your rights or request information, please contact us at `hurricane@blueshoe.io`.
